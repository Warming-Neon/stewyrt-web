2. The Audio Lifecycle — Special Category Data

⚠️ YOUR VOICE IS BIOMETRIC DATA

The vocal resonance analysis we perform constitutes processing of biometric data under Article 9 of UK GDPR — a special category that attracts the highest level of legal protection. We process this data solely to verify the demographic group you self-declare. We could not achieve equivalent demographic verification without it.

This processing occurs only with your explicit, separately given consent — via the dedicated consent step in the verification flow. You may withdraw that consent at any time by contacting privacy@stewyrt.com.

  • Lawful basis: Explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) UK GDPR) for biometric voice analysis; consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for all other processing.
  • Permanent Deletion: Your raw audio file is NEVER stored long-term. It is processed in volatile memory and purged immediately after the AI analysis is complete.
  • We retain only the anonymised demographic data points (age group, gender group, and sentiment classification) required to facilitate the Stewyrt experience.

3. What We Retain and For How Long

Different data has different lifespans on Stewyrt:

  • Raw audio recordings: Deleted immediately upon completion of AI processing — never stored.
  • Derived sentiment data (tone, flavour, demographic group): Retained indefinitely in truly anonymised, aggregated form. Because this data cannot be linked back to any individual by any means, it falls outside the scope of UK GDPR as anonymised information — not pseudonymised.
  • Session authentication data (local storage / essential cookie): Retained for the duration of your active session, or until you clear your browser storage.
  • Content moderation flags: Retained for 90 days for platform integrity purposes, then deleted.

5. Automated Decision-Making

⚠️ AI CLASSIFIES YOUR DEMOGRAPHIC GROUP

In accordance with Article 22 of UK GDPR, we disclose that Stewyrt uses automated processing — specifically Gemini AI — to classify your voice recording and place you into demographic groups (age range, gender group). This classification determines which segment of the Zeitgeist your submission contributes to.

This is automated decision-making. It is carried out with your explicit consent, and the output is a statistical interpretation — not an objective fact. The AI can and does make mistakes. You have the right to contest a classification by contacting us at hello@warmingneon.com, though note that by the time you do, the raw audio enabling re-analysis will have already been deleted.

7. AI Interpretation & Fallibility

⚠️ AI CAN AND DOES MAKE MISTAKES

Stewyrt utilises Gemini AI to analyse vocal resonance and sentiment. Like all AI systems, Gemini can make mistakes. All analysis is a statistical interpretation, not an objective fact. Results must not be used as evidence in legal, medical, or interpersonal disputes. The output is probabilistic, contextual, and inherently fallible.
