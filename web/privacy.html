2. The Audio Lifecycle

⚠️ TWO TYPES OF AUDIO — TWO DIFFERENT LIFECYCLES

Stewyrt records your voice in two distinct contexts, each with its own purpose and retention period.

2a. Verification Audio (Onboarding)

  • Purpose: To confirm you are a real human and not an automated bot. We check for the presence of continuous human speech. This recording is not used to identify you, estimate your age, gender, ethnicity, or any other personal characteristic.
  • Lawful basis: Consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR) — provided via the dedicated consent step in the verification flow.
  • Retention: Deleted immediately upon completion of AI processing. The file is never written to Firebase Storage — processing occurs in volatile Cloud Function memory only.
  • Your demographics (age, gender, ethnicity, region) are self-reported by you during onboarding and stored separately in your account record. They are never inferred from your voice.

2b. Sentiment Audio (Pulse Submissions)

  • Purpose: To generate a sentiment analysis (tone, flavour, essence) attached to a poll question.
  • Lawful basis: Consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR).
  • Retention: Stored in Firebase Storage (Google Cloud) for up to 120 days from the date of submission to allow content moderation review. An automated daily process permanently deletes files at the end of this window. Under GDPR Article 17 you may request early deletion by contacting social@stewyrt.com with the approximate date and time of your submission.
  • The derived sentiment classification (tone, flavour, essence) is retained after the audio is deleted, in truly anonymised form that cannot be linked back to you.

3. What We Retain and For How Long

Different data has different lifespans on Stewyrt:

  • Verification audio: Deleted immediately upon completion of AI processing — never written to Firebase Storage.
  • Sentiment audio (pulse submissions): Stored for up to 120 days from submission, then automatically and permanently deleted.
  • Derived sentiment data (tone, flavour, essence): Retained indefinitely in truly anonymised, aggregated form. Because this data cannot be linked back to any individual by any means, it falls outside the scope of UK GDPR as anonymised information — not pseudonymised.
  • Self-reported demographics (age, gender, ethnicity, region): Retained in your account record for as long as you hold an account. You may request deletion at any time.
  • Session authentication data (local storage / essential cookie): Retained for the duration of your active session, or until you clear your browser storage.
  • Content moderation flags: Retained for 90 days for platform integrity purposes, then deleted.

5. Automated Decision-Making

⚠️ AI PROCESSES YOUR VOICE AND CLASSIFIES YOUR SENTIMENT

In accordance with Article 22 of UK GDPR, we disclose that Stewyrt uses automated processing — specifically Gemini AI — in two ways:

  • Human verification: During onboarding, AI analyses your voice to determine whether it contains continuous human speech (bot detection). This is a binary pass/fail check — it does not classify your demographic group or produce any stored output beyond a verification flag on your account.
  • Sentiment classification: When you submit a pulse response, AI classifies the sentiment of your recording into tone, flavour, and essence categories. This classification determines how your submission contributes to Stewyrt's aggregated view.

Both processes are carried out with your explicit consent, and their outputs are statistical interpretations — not objective facts. The AI can and does make mistakes. You have the right to contest a sentiment classification by contacting us at social@stewyrt.com, though note that by the time you do, the audio enabling re-analysis may already have been deleted under the 120-day retention policy.

6. Against the Echo Chamber

Stewyrt encourages variety and cognitive diversity. We discourage using the platform to validate pre-existing opinions or to seek "legal standing" for a social consensus — that is not what this data is for.

7. AI Interpretation & Fallibility

⚠️ AI CAN AND DOES MAKE MISTAKES

Stewyrt utilises Gemini AI to analyse vocal resonance and sentiment. Like all AI systems, Gemini can make mistakes. All analysis is a statistical interpretation, not an objective fact. Results must not be used as evidence in legal, medical, or interpersonal disputes. The output is probabilistic, contextual, and inherently fallible.

11. How to Delete Your Data

You can delete your data directly within the application. Follow these steps:

  • Open Stewyrt on your device.
  • Tap the settings icon (located in the navigation bar).
  • Scroll down to the Account section and find Delete My Data.
  • Tap the button and confirm deletion in the dialog.

What gets deleted: Your anonymous profile and any responses linked to your account are permanently removed from our active database. Note that derived sentiment classifications (the emotion words and brain regions) are retained in truly anonymised, aggregated form as they cannot be linked back to you.

Audio Retention: Sentiment audio files are stored for up to 120 days via an automatic daily purge. These files are permanently deleted at the end of this window.
