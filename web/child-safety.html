1. Our Commitment

Warming Neon Ltd operates Stewyrt with a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and any content that endangers, exploits, or harms minors in any way.

Stewyrt is an anonymous audio sentiment platform. Users submit short voice recordings in response to rotating questions. All audio is processed by AI and subject to content moderation before any derived data is published to the platform.

Zero Tolerance: Any content that sexualises, exploits, or endangers children will result in immediate removal, permanent account suspension, and referral to relevant law enforcement authorities.

2. Age Restrictions

Stewyrt is strictly for users aged 18 years and older. This is enforced at onboarding:

  • Users must self-report their age group during onboarding — users under 18 are not permitted to proceed
  • Users must explicitly consent to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which state the 18+ requirement
  • Anonymous authentication means no verified identity — we rely on self-declaration and content moderation to enforce this boundary
  • Any user identified as a minor will have their session terminated and data deleted

3. Content Moderation

Every audio submission on Stewyrt passes through an automated content moderation pipeline before any derived sentiment data is published:

  • AI Moderation: All audio is analysed by Google Gemini AI, which includes content safety classification as part of the sentiment analysis pipeline
  • Automatic Blocking: Content flagged as violating our policies is automatically blocked and never published to the platform
  • Audio Retention: Voice recordings are retained for a maximum of 120 days then automatically purged — onboarding verification audio is deleted immediately after processing
  • No raw audio sharing: Raw audio files are never exposed to other users — only derived sentiment classifications (emotion words and brain region mappings) are published
  • Firestore Security Rules: All published data is locked behind server-side security rules — clients cannot write directly to the database

4. In-App Reporting Mechanisms

Stewyrt provides users with mechanisms to report content they believe violates our policies:

  • Long-press to report: Any response visible in the live feed can be reported by long-pressing it
  • Report categories: Users can report for harassment, hate speech, spam, misinformation, or other violations
  • Rate limiting: Report submissions are rate-limited to prevent abuse of the reporting system
  • Moderation review queue: All reports are logged to a secure moderation collection accessible only by Warming Neon Ltd administrators

To report a child safety concern directly: Email social@stewyrt.com with the subject line "Child Safety Report". We aim to respond within 24 hours.

5. How We Respond to Reports

Upon receiving a child safety report — whether in-app or via email — Warming Neon Ltd will:

  • Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours
  • Immediately review the reported content
  • Remove any content that violates our child safety standards
  • Permanently suspend the associated anonymous session
  • Preserve any evidence that may be required by law enforcement
  • Report to relevant authorities where legally required (see Section 6)

6. Reporting to Authorities

Warming Neon Ltd complies with all applicable UK and international laws regarding the mandatory reporting of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and child exploitation.

  • UK: Reports made to the Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) and the National Crime Agency (NCA)
  • International: Reports made to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline where applicable
  • Law enforcement cooperation: We will fully cooperate with law enforcement investigations and comply with all lawful requests for information

Legal obligation: Under the Online Safety Act 2023 and applicable UK law, we are legally required to report known CSAM to the relevant authorities. We take this obligation seriously.
