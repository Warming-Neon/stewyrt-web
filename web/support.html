1. Getting Help

Stewyrt is an anonymous audio sentiment platform. Because there are no traditional user accounts or profiles, getting help is simple. Most common questions and technical queries are answered in our FAQ section below.

Quick Help: If your question is not covered in the FAQs, you can reach out directly to our support team at support@stewyrt.com. We generally respond within 24 hours.

2. Frequently Asked Questions

What is Stewyrt?

Stewyrt is a platform for anonymous audio sentiment. It aggregates short voice recordings from real humans in response to daily questions to track collective emotional trends (the Zeitgeist).

How does the verification work?

To prevent bots and keep the community authentic, we use an automated onboarding verification flow. It records a short audio sample and uses Google Gemini AI to verify it is a real human voice. This is entirely anonymous and no documents are required.

Is my voice stored?

No. Your voice is used strictly to verify you are a human and to analyze the emotional tone of your response. All raw audio recordings are permanently deleted immediately after processing. We never keep archives of user voices.

Who can hear my response?

Nobody hears your raw voice recording. Other users only see the emotional tags (tone, flavor, essence) and a brief 3-to-4 word summary of what you said generated by our AI.

How is my data used?

The AI-derived emotional tags are aggregated into collective trends. Anonymised, aggregated trend data may be shared with academic researchers or public health organisations. No personal or identifying information is ever sold or shared.

Can I delete my responses?

Yes. You can delete any response you have submitted at any time by long-pressing it in the feed and selecting 'Remove my response'. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

What happens if I report content?

Reporting routes the response to a moderation queue for review. For zero-tolerance categories like harassment or hate speech, reported responses are immediately auto-blocked and hidden from the feed while our team reviews the reporter's claim.

Why was my response removed?

Submissions are automatically moderated or manually removed if they violate our Acceptable Use policy, which strictly prohibits threats of violence, hate speech, harassment, spam, and incitement to harm.

How do I delete all my data?

Because Stewyrt is fully anonymous and does not use sign-ups, we do not have user profile records. If you wish to purge all traces, you can delete your individual responses inside the app or email us at privacy@stewyrt.com for manual assistance.

4. Report a Problem

If you encounter offensive content, technical bugs, or other issues on Stewyrt, please report them using one of the channels below:

  • In-app reporting: Long-press any response item directly in the live feed to report it.
  • By email: Send details of the issue to support@stewyrt.com.
  • Child safety reporting: Contact us immediately at hello@warmingneon.com for any child safety matters. We respond 24/7.

Abuse Prevention: In-app reporting is monitored. Repeated fake or malicious reports may result in rate limiting or temporary suspension from submitting responses.
